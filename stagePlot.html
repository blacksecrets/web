Tech Rider: Black Secrets

A Tribute to Alice in Chains | Winchester, VA | Updated June 2025

Band Overview

Black Secrets is a dedicated tribute to the legendary grunge pioneers, Alice in Chains. Formed in 2024 in Winchester, VA, we bring a faithful and authentic recreation of the band’s iconic sound and intense live energy. Please contact our tech manager with any questions, contact information below.

Backline Requirements

  • Guitar Amps: 2x Marshall JCM800 or equivalent (100W, 4x12 cabs)
  • Bass Amp: Ampeg SVT-4 Pro with 8x10 cab or equivalent
  • Drum Kit: 5-piece (22" kick, 12"/14"/16" toms, 14" snare) - Pearl, Tama, or DW preferred
  • Cymbals: 2x crash (16"/18"), 1x ride (20"), 1x hi-hat (14") - Zildjian or Sabian
  • Band provides own guitar pedals and cables

Lighting & Effects

  • Lighting: Dim red/blue washes, white spotlight for vocal/guitar solos
  • Effects: Light haze/fog for grunge atmosphere (no strobes)
  • Backdrops: Band logo or neutral black backdrop

Power Requirements

  • 6x 120V outlets: 2 stage right (guitar), 2 stage left (bass), 2 drum riser
  • Stable power supply for guitar pedals and amps

Hospitality

  • 12x bottled water (room temp)
  • 4x clean towels
  • Small green room for pre/post-show prep
  • Optional: Coffee, light snacks (fruit, chips)
